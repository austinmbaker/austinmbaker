About

Austin Baker is a product leader who has spent 24 years deciding what to build next in places where that decision was hard.

I came up through design and founded companies, but the work has always been the same underneath: figure out what matters, decide what to build, and see it through in environments where the problems are complex and the stakes are real. Healthcare, defense, consumer tech. The industries change. The kind of problem doesn't.

What Austin believes

  1. Talk to users before you make assumptions.

    The fastest way to build the wrong thing is to decide you already know. The work starts with the people who'll use it.

  2. Research should turn into results, and that only happens when decisions get made.

    Insight that doesn't become a decision is just a nice report. The job is to make the call, informed and quickly, without waiting for the perfect scenario.

  3. If we want a better future, we have to be the ones who build it.

    That means calculated risks and moving before the path is fully clear. Staying on the forefront of what's coming isn't a career tactic. It's the actual work.
