The reframe

Most patient portals are built around the data: records, results, messages, bills. But the patients who need Mayo most are in the middle of something complex and high-stakes, and a better filing cabinet doesn't help them. So we reframed the portal as a guide, something that helps patients and the people caring for them understand where they are, what's next, and what to do.

That shift changed the questions the team asked. Instead of "where does this feature go," it became "what does this person need right now." It also brought caregivers into the picture, the family members and partners who often carry the logistics of someone's care but were invisible to the product. Getting there inside a large organization meant running two tracks at once. We think in the now, the near, and the far, shipping value continuously while building the foundation for where the product needs to go.
