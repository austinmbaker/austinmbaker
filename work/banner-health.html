Banner Health

Banner asked for a way to find other physicians. What doctors really needed was a way to reach them.

Role
Senior Product Designer (principal-level role), Tallwave, embedded with Banner Health
Scope
Clinician interviews, requirements, design, and implementation with engineering for a provider platform serving 10,000 doctors
Result
Provider Central, a HIPAA-compliant app for finding, messaging, and coordinating with physicians across the Banner network

The reframe

Banner came to Tallwave wanting a provider-only app that made it easier to find other physicians in their network, for referrals and for the quick, informal conversations doctors call curbside consults. When we interviewed clinical staff, search turned out to be only half the problem. Finding the right physician was one step. Reaching them was the harder one. Without a direct number, a doctor had to go through the front office, and every delay there was a delay in someone's care.

So the product became two things working together. A provider search with the filters doctors actually needed, including whether someone was accepting new patients, and secure messaging that let them reach out casually without going through formal channels. As it grew, it took on more of what doctors needed to coordinate care, like HR information and on-call schedules, so a primary care physician could check in with the on-call doctor on how their patient was doing in the hospital. I led most of the requirements gathering with clinicians, owned the design, and worked with the engineers through implementation. Banner had brought Tallwave in for people who could work with a high degree of autonomy, and that's how this project ran.
